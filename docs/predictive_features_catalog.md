# Initial Catalog of Candidate Predictive Features for Arcora

This catalog documents the initial set of engineered features identified during Week 1 Exploratory Data Analysis for Week 2+ feature engineering and game prediction models (win probability classification and home/away score regression).

| # | Feature Name | Category | Target(s) | Lag / Window | Source Entities | Rationale |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `rolling_net_rating_5g_10g` | Team Form & Efficiency | `point_margin, home_win` | Last 5 and 10 games prior to tip-off (strictly lagged) | `games` | Rolling point differential captures short- and medium-term team strength and is strongly predictive of upcoming game margins. |
| 2 | `elo_rating_differential` | Long-Term Team Strength | `home_win, point_margin` | Cumulative across seasons with offseason mean reversion | `games` | Dynamic Elo ratings adjust for opponent quality and margin of victory, providing a calibrated baseline win probability. |
| 3 | `rolling_efg_pct_differential` | Shooting Efficiency (Four Factors) | `home_pts, away_pts, home_win` | Last 10 games (expanding window early in season) | `games`, `player_game_stats` | Effective field goal percentage (eFG% = (FGM + 0.5 * FG3M) / FGA) weights 3-point efficiency and has the highest correlation with winning. |
| 4 | `rest_days_and_back_to_back_flag` | Schedule & Fatigue | `home_pts, away_pts, point_margin` | Days elapsed since each team's previous game_date | `games` | Teams playing on 0 days rest (second night of a back-to-back), especially on the road, suffer a measurable drop in shooting efficiency and win rate. |
| 5 | `home_away_location_split_win_pct` | Venue & Home-Court Advantage | `home_win, point_margin` | Season-to-date home win % vs away win % | `games` | Home teams win 59.0% of historical games (+3.01 mean margin); team-specific venue splits capture altitude (e.g., DEN, UTA) and crowd effects. |
| 6 | `rolling_pace_and_total_possessions` | Pace & Tempo | `total_pts, home_pts, away_pts` | Last 10 games prior to tip-off | `games`, `player_game_stats` | Estimated possessions (~FGA - OREB + TO + 0.44 * FTA) directly govern total game scoring volume across different NBA eras. |
| 7 | `rolling_rebound_and_assist_margin` | Ball Movement & Possession Control | `point_margin, home_win` | Last 10 games prior to tip-off | `games`, `player_game_stats` | In-game rebound differential (r = +0.405 with point_margin) and assist differential (r = +0.478 with point_margin) are strong structural drivers. |
| 8 | `active_starters_minutes_and_pie_availability` | Roster & Player Availability | `home_win, point_margin` | Rolling 10-game top-5 rotation player impact | `player_game_stats`, `players` | Aggregating recent scoring and plus/minus of available starters accounts for key player injuries, trades, and load management. |
| 9 | `three_point_attempt_rate_3par` | Modern Spacing & Shot Profile | `total_pts, home_pts, away_pts` | Season-to-date and rolling 10-game FG3A / FGA | `player_game_stats` | Captures secular offensive evolution (league 3PAr rose from 18.8% in 2003 to 39.2% in 2021-22) and variance in scoring outcomes. |
| 10 | `head_to_head_season_series_margin` | Matchup History | `point_margin, home_win` | Trailing 3-season head-to-head matchups | `games` | Captures stylistic matchup advantages between specific franchises and division rivals. |
